## How to use and maintain

- Choose the closest row only when scope, risk, technical surface and validation burden are comparable. If no row fits, follow current risk tiers; an isolated low-risk unknown family may be piloted with Luna and proportionate validation. Medium shared integration generally Terra unless a narrowly scoped, user-directed pilot justifies otherwise. Hard/security/critical work stays under Sol and required actual Supervisor gates.
- Compare accepted outcomes, not output claims or model reputation. A self-reported success, partial check, blocked environment/provider call, missing review, or merely passing build remains provisional/incomplete as specified in its source record. Provider/approval/environment blockage alone is not a model-quality failure and does not demote a model.
- Update the relevant row as part of the original executor's ordinary closeout after a real accepted or failed comparable task. Add exact evidence IDs, acceptance basis/gate, corrections or takeover, evidence completeness, sample limitations, and actual exposed runtime/counters; use “unknown” when absent. Do not spawn agents or a campaign just to collect statistics.
- Learn price-quality only from comparable actual cost to accepted result, including corrective work and evidence overhead where measured. Keep unknowns unknown: no invented tokens/prices, universal percentages, arbitrary quotas, or model-wide claims. Child durations are not full wall time; do not sum overlapping phases. Included/zero tool billing does not establish zero total cost.
- This matrix is advisory, consulted before dispatch; it does not automatically select a model, change routing/configuration, relax existing gates, or replace injected live instructions.
